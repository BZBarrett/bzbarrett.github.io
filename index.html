About

I am an Assistant Professor in the Department of Geography and Sustainability at the University of Tennessee, Knoxville. My research sits at the intersection of GeoAI, Responsible GIScience, and disaster resilience, leveraging large language models, social media, and geospatial big data to support smart disaster response and equitable community recovery.

Prior to joining UTK, I was an Assistant Teaching Professor at Pennsylvania State University and completed my Ph.D. at Texas A&M University under the advisement of Prof. Lei Zou. I also bring more than six years of applied geospatial engineering experience from industry.
